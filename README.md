# Memoir - A Photo Journaling and Sharing App

When something momentous, exciting, or amusing happens, I often capture it in a quick photo. When I scroll back through my camera roll, it's not hard for me to imagine it as snapshots of my life (meeting new people, travelling to new places, trying new food, and spending time with people who mean a lot to me). Unfortunately, I often forget a lot of the details and when I look through my photos I can't help but feel like something is missing.

Memoir is a photo journaling app that helps you remember and share the photo-worthy moments of your life. At the end of each day, it shows you the photos you took and prompts you to annotate them with a journal entry about what you were doing.

### Using Memoir you can:
- Annotate photos with text descriptions and tags.
- Organize photos into folders and albums.
- Backup your photos to your other devices.
- Save space on a device by loading in photos from your other devices on-demand.
- Share albums with friends.
- Search for images based on description, people in the image, tags, location, photographer, and more.

### Networking
Memoire uses peer to peer networking to connect your devices and your friends devices. This means your images are never stored on a centeral server and you don't have to pay for an expensive cloud store subscription.
